# MTG-Jamendo attribution and licensing

The unchanged `raw_30s_cleantags.tsv` snapshot and the MTG-Jamendo metadata fields
in the recovered manifest originate from the Music Technology Group's
[MTG-Jamendo dataset](https://github.com/MTG/mtg-jamendo-dataset).

under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).
The manifest selects and rearranges metadata rows and adds recovery information
and checksums. Those changes were made on 2026-10-28. Retain the upstream terms
for the metadata and its adaptations. This material is distinct from the
author-created AImoclips dataset and its CC BY 4.0 license.

Citation: Bogdanov, D., Won, M., Tovstogan, P., Porter, A., and Serra, X. (2019).
The MTG-Jamendo Dataset for Automatic Music Tagging. Machine Learning for Music
Discovery Workshop, ICML 2019. https://hdl.handle.net/10230/42015

Reference audio is not included. The manifest points to original distribution
URLs. Individual audio licenses remain with their respective rightsholders;
see the upstream `audio_licenses.txt`. CLAP weights are also not included.
